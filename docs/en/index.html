Collect gems, buy cards, race to 15 points

Gem Merchant's Workshop is a site for learning a 2-player card game, where you collect gem chips, buy cards, and grow stronger bit by bit through card discounts, by playing against a strong AI.

Play the AI

Play the AIFour levels from Easy to Strongest. Hints show how the AI would play, with your winning chance. Strong AI tipsDiscounts early, points late. Tips checked against numbers from AI-vs-AI games. Next-move quizPuzzles solved by reading the endgame to the end. Find the move that wins. By the numbersFrom strong AI-vs-AI games: first-player edge, how points are scored, and whether points or cards lead mid-game. How to playThe 4 things you can do on a turn, discounts, nobles and how it ends, with pictures.

The AI on this site

The AI tries many guesses of the hidden cards (deck order and your blind reserves) and reads ahead with Information Set Monte Carlo Tree Search. It judges the positions it reaches with an evaluation learned from AI self-play. It runs inside your browser, so no connection or sign-up is needed.

Research notes are on GitHub . It plays like the existing gem card game, but the name, art and card contents were made for this site.
