7 tips from a strong AI

Tips found in numbers from 1,000 games between strong AIs (fmcts2000). Every tip is checked against the numbers before it is shown (the numbers are on the data page).

1Cards (discounts) early, points late

At round 10, the player with more cards went on to win 68% of the time, and the player with more points 54%. By round 25 it flips: more points 74%, more cards 61%. Early on, build discounts even with 0-point cards; once your opponent nears 15 points, switch to moves that score.

2Level 2 cards are the backbone

Of the winner's 15.6 points, 9.1 come from level 2 cards. The shape the AI buys most is “2 pts, 7 gems (4＋2＋1)”. Once you have discounts in 3–4 colors, aim for a position where you can buy level 2 cards one after another.

3Level 3 is the card that decides

Winners buy 1.0 level 3 cards and losers 0.4. For level 1 it's 6.1 vs 6.1, no real difference. Whether you can buy one level 3 card, made cheap by discounts late in the game, decides the result.

4Reserve the cards you'll buy later

The AI reserves 5.0 times per player per game and later buys 48% of those cards. Only 8% are blind reserves from a deck. The main use is to reserve a visible card you can buy once your discounts line up, and take the gold too.

5Usually take 3 gems of different colors

Of moves that take gems, 8% take 2 of one color. Taking 3 in one move is better, so 2 of a color is for when you're short exactly 2 of that color.

6Nobles come along, don't chase them

Winners get 0.51 nobles on average (losers 0.11), worth 1.5 points. Rather than lining up discount colors for a noble, the strong way is to buy the cards you want and let the discounts drift toward a noble's requirement.

7Don't hoard chips

Only 12% of turns had to return chips for going over 10. Returning wastes a turn's worth of gems, so at 9–10 chips switch to buying or reserving.
