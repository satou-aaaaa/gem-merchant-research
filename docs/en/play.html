    How to play (short)
    • On your turn, do one: take 3 gems of different colors / take 2 of a color with 4+ / reserve a card and take 1 gold / buy a card.
    • A bought card is a 1-gem discount in its color (for good). Some cards also have points.
    • You may hold up to 10 chips; return any excess. Gold stands in for any color.
    • When your discounts meet a noble's requirement, the noble visits at the end of your turn for 3 points.
    • When someone reaches 15 points, finish the round and the game ends. Most points wins (on a tie, fewer cards bought wins).
