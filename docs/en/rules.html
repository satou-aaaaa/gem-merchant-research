How to play

Reaching 15 points first matters, but you can't buy expensive cards right away. Build discounts with cheap cards, then work up to costlier ones: that is the flow of the game.

Components

  • Gem chips in 5 colors (W Bu G R Bk) ×4 each, and gold, which stands in for any color, Au ×5.
  • 90 cards (40 level 1, 30 level 2, 20 level 3). Lay 4 of each level face up; the rest form the decks.
  • 3 nobles (out of 10).

What you can do on a turn (pick one)

What to doContents
Take 3 different colorsOne gem each of 3 different colors from the bank. If 2 or fewer colors remain, take what there is.
Take 2 of one colorOnly if that color has 4 or more before taking.
ReserveTake a face-up card or the top card of a deck (face down) into your hand and get 1 gold. Up to 3 reserves. Only you can buy a reserved card.
BuyOne card from the board or your reserves. Pay its cost to the bank; gold can cover any shortfall.

Discounts (the heart of the game)

A card you buy counts as a 1-gem discount in its color for the rest of the game. For example, with 2 Bu cards, every card's blue cost is 2 cheaper. The more discounts you have, the fewer turns you spend collecting chips, and the sooner you reach high-value cards.

10 chips max

If you have more than 10 chips (gold included) at the end of your turn, return any you like to the bank to get down to 10.

Nobles

Each noble asks for a set number of discounts in certain colors. If you meet it at the end of your turn, that noble visits for 3 points. It costs no action. Only one visits per turn, and you can't refuse.

End of game and winner

If anyone has 15 or more points at the end of a turn, finish the round (through the second player's turn) and the game ends. Most points wins. On a tie, whoever bought fewer cards wins.

Tips for beginners

  • Start by building discounts with cheap level 1 cards. Even with 0 points, a discount lasts all game.
  • Don't pile up one color; build discounts in the colors expensive cards need.
  • If your opponent is one move from buying an expensive card, you can reserve it to block them.
