Who has the edge, first or second player?

The first player wins 48.4% (95% range 45.4%〜51.5%). No clear first-player edge. Draws: 9. With a gap this small, either seat can win.

How long a game lasts

Counting one move by each player as a round, a game lasts 30.0 rounds on average (median 29, shortest 22). 13 games were stopped at 100 rounds because nobody could buy and players only took and returned gems. The first card with points is bought around round 7.9 on average, and the first level 3 card around round 23.0.

Mid-game, does leading in points or in cards win?

The share of games won by the player ahead in points, or ahead in cards (discounts), at the end of that round. At round 10,More cards (discounts) is more often the winner.But by round 25 it flips, and the player ahead in points wins more often. Build cards (discounts) early, then switch to points late: the numbers show this flow.
